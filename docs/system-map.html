ADAM System Map

ADAM is a small AI that runs entirely on your laptop. It reasons over facts it can prove, answers only from evidence, and remembers everything. This page shows every part of it in plain words, what each stage changed, every decision and who made it, and what is still open.

How to read this map

Parts are boxes

Each box in the architecture is one part of ADAM. Click it to see what it does, in plain words first and in technical terms after.

Colours are recent changes

Green is new in Phase 4, amber changed in Phase 4, grey unchanged, blue dashed kept for a future job, red removed.

Code versus data

Boxes above “On disk” are programs. Boxes in “On disk” are the files those programs write: your actual data.
